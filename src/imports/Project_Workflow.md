#### ***Warehouse Management System - Social welfare, probation \& Child care service Department***



* ##### ***Workflow***



1. ###### ***Organizational Structure***



The department is divided into two primary divisions with two separate stores, each containing specific sections:

* **Social Services Division**



&#x09;**-** Institutional Section

&#x09;- Development Section

&#x09;- Accounts Section



* **Probation and Child Care Division**



&#x09;**-** Institutional Section

&#x09;- Development Section

&#x09;- Probation Section



***Note: Each division maintains its own independent Physical Store, but both must be managed separately through a unified digital system.***

###### 

###### ***2. Stationery Request \& Recommendation Process***



* Frequency: Users are permitted to request goods once a month.
* Workflow: When a Development Officer requests stationery, a designated Higher Officer from their specific section must recommend the request.
* every user can request the goods(commissioner, assistant commissioner, assistant director, AO, accountant, section heads store keeper and other low level officers who use the system) once a month.
* assistant commissioner, assistant director, AO, accountant and section heads need the commissioner's approval for the good issuance. No need of recommendation.
* commissioner do not need any approval. and she can get the relevant goods from both the stores. others get the goods from their relevant division store.



***Recommendation Authority Matrix:***

|-|-|-|
|**Social Services**|Institutional|Chief Clerk (CC)|
||Development|CC|
||Accounts|Financial Assistant (FA)|

###### ***3. Approval \& Modification Power***



Once recommended, the request must be Approved by one of the following high-ranking officers:

* Commissioner / Assistant Commissioner
* Assistant Director
* Administrative Officer
* Accountant



**Key Feature:** These Higher Officers have the authority to edit request details (such as reducing the quantity) before final approval.



###### ***4. Fulfillment \& Issuance***



* Once approved, the request is automatically forwarded to the Store Keeper.
* The Store Keeper is responsible for the final issuance of the goods.
* when the goods arrived at the store from the suppliers that details should add to the system.(supplier's name, date...)



###### ***5. Access Control \& Special Cases***



* **Division Constraints:** Most staff members are restricted to requesting stationery only from their assigned division.
* **Universal Access:** The Commissioner has the privilege to request stationery from both stores/divisions. She can access the both stores.
* **External Institutions (Children's Homes, etc.):**

&#x09;- These entities should have their own system accounts.

&#x09;- there are 19 institutions. 17 for **Probation and Child Care Division.** other 2 for **Social Services Division.**

* There are 50 divisional secretary offices for each division(social service and probation \& child service). they also can request goods. their request must be recommended by the Administrative officer. can be approved by a higher officer. 
* Recommending officers(CC/FA) can request goods for their section for the common usage for the section staff members. those requests cannot be added to their(CC/FA) personal records. those requests/records have to be managed seperately.

* ###### ***functions required from the system***



&#x09;- items registration(item name, item category, item code, units, started quantity)

&#x09;- stock receiving(item name, received quantity, received date, supplier, GRN number)

&#x09;- stock issue(item name, issued quantity, issued section, issued officer, issued date)

&#x09;- identify the items using bar code(item details, current quantity, item history)

&#x09;- stock balance monitoring(manage reorder level)

&#x09;- stock movement tracking(note down the transaction history from receiving goods to the inventory to issuing goods)

&#x09;- reporting system(current quantity, issued items reports, received items reports, monthly/annual reports)(can view reports )

&#x09;- user management(admin, commissioner, assistant commissioner, assistant director, accountant, administrative officer, section officers, store keeper, officers(who are requesting goods))

&#x09;- security and access control(newly registered accounts must verify by commissioner only)

&#x09;- online web based access(mobile friendly)
